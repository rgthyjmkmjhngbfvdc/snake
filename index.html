Arcade-Ready Controls

Switch between manual and assisted play instantly. Queue moves, dash through tight turns, and let the AI lend a hand when the rival gets too fierce.

Smart Rival AI

A dedicated rival snake plots safe paths using breadth-first search, simulating future states to avoid traps and keep the pressure on your run.

Glow-infused tiles, layered fruit sprites, and animated heads bring the arena to life, whether you are practicing or streaming the ultimate duel.

Battle-Tested Strategy

The rival studies every move, ensuring the board always feels alive. Advanced pathfinding keeps both snakes darting toward fruit, while a safety check prevents reckless dives into corners.
